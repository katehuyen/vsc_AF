# Explore the guide

The built-in guide (**Agent Farm: Open Guide**) is the handbook for the whole system — English by default, with Türkçe selectable from the language toggle inside the guide panel:

- **Recipe list** — "what do you want to do?" → the right section
- **25 scenarios** (the Examples document) — from the inbox factory to the media production pipeline
- **Media pipeline** — image-video production with fal.ai/Replicate, gallery, scene board, the Premiere bridge
- **Webhook bridge** — the n8n/Telegram two-way line
- **Release notes** — when and why each new capability arrived

Every `?` button in the panel drops to the relevant section of the guide.
